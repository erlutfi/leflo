Leflo Support

Questions, problems, or ideas? Write to ltfstudio@outlook.com. We usually reply within a few days.

My notes don't appear on my other device

Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is turned on in Settings. Then tap the sync button at the bottom left of the main screen. The first sync can take a few minutes.

How do I restore my Leflo Pro subscription?

Open Settings in Leflo and tap Restore Subscription. The subscription belongs to your Apple Account, so it works on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the App Store, tap your account picture, then Subscriptions. You can also open Settings in Leflo and tap Manage Subscription. Your notebooks stay available after the subscription ends.

I deleted something by mistake

Deleted pages and notebooks wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Open Settings in Leflo, then Recently Deleted, to bring them back.

Will I lose my notes if I delete the app?

If iCloud is on, your notes stay in your iCloud account and come back when you reinstall. If iCloud is off, notes are only on that device and are removed with the app.
