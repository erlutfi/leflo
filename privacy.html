Leflo Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Leflo is a notebook app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your notes are yours, and we never see them.

What we collect

Nothing. Leflo has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking tools. We don't collect, store, or sell any information about you or how you use the app.

Where your notes are stored

Your notebooks, pages, handwriting, typed text, and images are stored on your device.

If you're signed in to iCloud, Leflo also keeps them in your private iCloud database so they stay in sync across your devices. That database belongs to your Apple account. Apple stores it under its own privacy policy, and we have no way to read it.

If you aren't signed in to iCloud, your notes stay only on the device where you created them.

Photos and files

When you add a photo or image to a page, Leflo saves a copy of that image inside your notebook. Leflo can only see the photos and files you pick. It doesn't have access to the rest of your photo library or your files.

Purchases

Leflo Pro is an optional subscription sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, card, or billing details. Apple only tells the app whether a subscription is active on your Apple Account. You can manage or cancel it anytime in your App Store account settings.

TestFlight

If you use a beta version of Leflo through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with us. Apple collects this information under the TestFlight terms, and we use it only to fix problems in the app.

Deleting your data

Deleting a notebook or page moves it to Recently Deleted, where it's permanently removed after 30 days. You can also remove it right away from Recently Deleted. Deleting the app removes the notes stored on that device. To remove synced notes from iCloud, go to Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage on your device.

Children

Leflo doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
